## 🚀 Featured Projects

### ⚖️ Nyaya AI

**Problem:**  
Indian court judgments and legal documents are often lengthy, technical and difficult for non-legal users to understand.

**What it does:**  
Nyaya AI is an AI-powered legal assistant that processes Indian court documents and converts complex legal content into simpler explanations. It also allows users to ask questions directly based on uploaded documents.

**Key Features:**
- 📄 PDF and legal document processing
- 🔍 Document-based question answering
- 🤖 AI-powered legal content simplification
- 🌐 Multilingual explanation support
- 🔊 Text-to-speech output
- 💬 Conversational interaction with documents

**Tech:** `Python` `FastAPI` `React` `AI/ML` `PDF Processing` `TTS`

---

### 💳 CardSync AI

**Problem:**  
Manually entering contact information from physical business cards is time-consuming and can lead to incorrect or duplicated data.

**What it does:**  
CardSync AI is an OCR-based business card management system that extracts contact information from physical and digital business cards, validates the extracted data and stores it in a structured format.

**Key Features:**
- 📸 Business card image scanning
- 🔎 OCR-based text extraction
- 🧹 Image preprocessing for better recognition
- ✉️ Email and phone number detection
- ✅ Extracted data validation
- 👤 Contact management
- 🔗 Integration-ready contact storage

**Tech:** `Python` `FastAPI` `OpenCV` `OCR` `PostgreSQL` `React`

### 🪙 Physical Gold Resale Forecasting

**Problem:**  
The resale value of physical gold depends on changing market prices, making it difficult to estimate when the resale value may recover the original purchase cost.

**What it does:**  
This machine-learning research system uses historical gold-price data and time-series forecasting to estimate future gold prices and determine the approximate break-even timeline for physical gold resale.

**Key Features:**
- 📊 Historical gold-price analysis
- 📈 Time-series forecasting
- 🔮 Future gold-price prediction
- 💰 Break-even timeline estimation
- 📉 Forecast visualization
- 📋 Research-oriented model evaluation

**Tech:** `Python` `Machine Learning` `SARIMA` `Time-Series Analysis`

🏆 **Best Paper Award — DIGISF'26**

## 🔬 Research

### 🏆 AI-Based Gold Resale Break-Even Forecasting

**Research Problem:**  
Gold resale decisions are influenced by fluctuating market prices, making it difficult to estimate how long it may take for the resale value of physical gold to reach the original investment cost.

**Research Focus:**  
Developing a predictive framework that combines historical gold-price analysis and time-series forecasting to estimate future prices and calculate the expected break-even period for physical gold resale.

**Core Areas:**  
`Time-Series Forecasting` `Machine Learning` `Predictive Analytics` `Gold Price Analysis`

🏆 **Best Paper Award — DIGISF'26**

### 🏆 Missing Person Detection Using Multimodal AI

**Research Problem:**  
A person's appearance can change significantly over time because of factors such as age, hairstyle, clothing and environmental conditions. This makes identifying missing persons from older or different images challenging.

**Research Focus:**  
Developing a multimodal AI approach for identifying missing persons by combining multiple forms of visual information and handling appearance and time-related variations.

**Core Areas:**  
`Multimodal AI` `Computer Vision` `Image Analysis` `Deep Learning` `Person Identification`
